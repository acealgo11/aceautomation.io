Questions you're probably asking right now

We've answered the most common ones here so your call is focused on your specific situation — not the basics.

1
What exactly is Ace Algo and how does it work?
Ace Algo is a fully automated AI trading algorithm that runs inside your own regulated brokerage account. It scans and executes trades across multi-trillion dollar liquid markets — simultaneously combining technical price analysis with real-world market fundamentals at a speed no human trader could match. You open an account, fund it, connect the algo, and it runs. You do nothing. Every trade is visible to you in real time. Profits compound directly in your account.
2
Is my money safe? Who holds it?
Your capital is held entirely in your own account at a regulated broker. We never hold, touch, or have access to your money at any point. You have full visibility over every trade and can withdraw to your bank or crypto wallet at any time without permission or lockup periods.
3
What does the onboarding process look like?
After your call, if you decide to move forward, you'll open a regulated brokerage account — our team guides you through the 10-minute setup. Once funded, the algo is connected via API and goes live within 24 hours. From that point you don't need to do anything. The team monitors performance and you can check your account whenever you like.
4
What are the realistic returns — including bad months?
Live tracked performance since 2021 shows 5–17% monthly returns depending on the risk profile chosen. We include the full data — not just the good months. There have been months with smaller returns and, rarely, flat periods. The system is built around capital protection first: it cuts losing trades fast and does not hold positions hoping for a reversal. Consistent compounding over time is the goal, not chasing moonshots.
5
Is this long-term? Can I use it for income, saving for the future or retirement?
Yes — and this is one of the most common reasons people join. The algo compounds continuously, so the longer it runs the more powerful it becomes. Members use it in different ways: some withdraw monthly profits as a passive income stream, others let it compound untouched to build long-term wealth or supplement an IRA/401(k). Unlike a traditional retirement account, your money is accessible at any time — there is no age restriction, no lockup, and no waiting until 59½. You stay in full control of when and how you use the returns.
6
Do I need any trading experience?
None whatsoever. The majority of our members came in with little or no trading background. Some had tried manual trading before and lost money. None of that matters here — you are not here to trade, you are here to let a machine do it for you. Your only job is to let it run. The algo handles everything else.
